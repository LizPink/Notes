## 请求/信息类
 > **Prompt (请求复习资料):**
 > You are taking a university psychology course. Your professor has invited students to attend an optional review session before the final exam. You would like to attend, but the session is scheduled at the same time as another required class.
>
> Write an email to your professor. In your email:
> - explain the scheduling conflict;
> - explain why you would like to attend the review session;
> - ask whether there is another way to access the review material and explain how you would use it to prepare for the exam.
>
> **Answer:**
> Dear Professor Smith,
> I hope you are doing well. My name is Li and I am a student in your psychology class. I am writing to let you know about a scheduling conflict. 
> 
> *I would very much like to* attend the review session because it would help me review the key concepts, and *identify the topics that I have not fully understood* before the final exam. However, I just learned that the session is scheduled at the same time as another required class I have to attend.
> 
> Therefore, I would like to ask whether there is another way to access the review materials. *Once I receive them, I will* carefully go over the notes so that I do not miss the main points of the review. This would help me prepare more effectively for the exam.
> 
> Thank you for your consideration and help.
> Best regards,
> Li

>**Prompt（请求旅游计划）:**
You are planning a business trip to another city. Your colleague, Maria, recently traveled to the same city for work. You want to ask her recommendations for places to eat and things to do during your free time.
>
Write an email to Maria. In your email, do the following.
• Explain why you are traveling to the city.
• Ask for her recommendations about restaurants and activities.
• Mention what you'd most like to do and what you'd like to avoid.
>
**Answer:**
Dear Maria,
I am planning to travel to the city next week for a business trip. I will be attending several meetings with my client, but I should have some free time in the evenings.
>
Since you recently traveled there for work, I am wondering if you could recommend some good restaurants and interesting places to visit. *I would especially appreciate suggestions for* *local restaurants*, since I would like to *try some food that is unique to the city.* If there are any museums, parks *or other attractions worth visiting*, *I would also be interested in hearing about them*.
>
Personally, *I would most like* to explore the local culture and try some traditional food. However, I would prefer to avoid places that are extremely crowded or too far from the city center because I will not have much free time.
Best regards,
Li

**案例点评：**
结构上，这篇文章的行文结构为：（1）表达目的+（2）请求推荐+（3）个人喜好。

表达上，在谈及推荐的时候，可以说：
- I would especially appreciate suggestions for ... . If there are any ..., I would also be interested in hearing about them. （我想了解...同时我也想了解...）

在谈及个人喜好的时候，可以说：
- I would most like to ... / I would very much like to ...（我个人喜欢做的事情）
- explore local culture and try some traditional food（探索当地文化、品尝传统美食）

在谈及我不喜欢的事情的时候，也可以说：
- However, I would prefer not to .../ I would prefer to avoid ...

>**Prompt（小组任务问题处理）:**
>You have been working on a group project with several classmates for a course. One of your group members, Alex, has not been participating actively and has missed several assignments. You want to address this issue and find a solution. Write an email to Alex. In your email, do the following.
>- Describe the current progress of the group project.
>- Suggest ways he can contribute to the project moving forward.
>
>**Answer:**
>Dear Alex,
>I am writing to *update you on the progress of our group project* and discuss how we can work together more effectively.
>
>So far, we have finished most of the research and *completed the first draft of the presentation slides*. We are now working on the final slides and preparing for the presentation. However, since you have missed several assignments, the rest of the group members *have had to take on some additional work.*
>
>*There are still several ways you can contribute.* For example, you can *help revise the presentation slides* and check the information for any mistakes. You could also *prepare one section of the presentation and present it during our final presentation*. This would help us divide the remaining work more fairly.
>
>Please let me know which task you would prefer to take. I hope we can finish the project successfully together.
>Best regards,
>Li

案例点评：
结构上，这篇文章的结构是`问题描述`+`解决方案`。
表达上，可以积累一些团队协作中的常用表达
- update you on the progress of our project （同步一下最新的项目进展）
- the first draft of our presentation slides（第一版本的PPT展示）
针对团队协作出现的问题，可以这么描述
- since you have missed several assignment, the other members have had to take on additional work. （因为你的缺席，团队其他成员已经承担了不少的额外任务）

针对团队协作中出现的问题，可以这么描述解决方法
-  There are still some ways you can contribute to the project. For example, you could ... You could also ...（你仍然有机会参与到团队贡献中来，比如说你可以...或者你可以...）

在解决方案的描述中，除了讲述具体的描述细节，通常会在最后加上一些效果的总结，例如
- This would help us divide the remaining work more fairly. （这样我们的后续分工会更加合理）

## 建议/推荐类
**Prompt（餐馆的反馈以及后续建议）:**
> Your coworker, Kevin, recently recommended a new restaurant for your team to visit. You took the team there, but everyone was disappointed. The food was not as good as expected, and the service was slow. You need to inform Kevin about the situation and discuss future lunch options.
>
> Write an email to Kevin. In your email, do the following.
>• Explain what was wrong with the restaurant.
>• Describe the team's reaction to the visit.
>• Suggest alternative lunch arrangements.

**Answer:**
>Dear kevin,
>Thank you again for recommending a restaurant for our team lunch. Unfortunately, *our experience there was not as good as we had expected*.
>
>The main problem was the food. *Several dishes were not very fresh*, and *the portions were smaller than we expected*. In addition, the service was quited slow. *We had to wait for almost forty minutes before our food arrived*, which was inconvenient *because some team members needed to return to work*.
>
>Most of the team members were disappointed with the experience. *A few said they would prefer not to visit the restaurant again.*
>
>For future team lunches, I think *either* we choose a place that we have visited before *or* *ask everyone to suggest a few options and then vote for their favorite*. This might help us find a place that everyone enjoys.
>Best,
>Li

**案例点评**
这篇文章写的很好。
在结构上，本文采取的是`问题描述`+`客观评价`+`解决思路`的结构。

在表达上，关于评价一个餐厅的不足之处，我们可以这么说：
- The food was the main problem. Several dishes were not fresh and the portions were smaller than we expected. (吃的一般。食物不够新鲜，并且分量还不多。)
- The service was slow. We had to wait for forty minutes before our food arrived. (服务业一般，我们等了好久才等到上菜。)

同时在最后的建议部分，建议也可以采用either ... or的分支结构展开。关于让大家投票选择最好的方案也可以这么说：
- I suggest we either choose a restaurant we visited before or ask everyone *to suggest a few options and then vote for their favorite*.

**Prompt（公园设备的维护建议）:**
> You are a member of the neighborhood association, and you have noticed that the local park needs some maintenance. You decide to contact the association president, Mr. Williams, to discuss the issues and offer a suggestion. Write an email to Mr. Williams. In your email, do the following: 
>- Describe the maintenance issue you have observed in the park.
>- Explain why it is important to address the issue.
>- Make a suggestion for how to deal with the issue.
>
>**Answer:**
>Dear Mr. Williams,
>I am writing to report a maintenance issue I noticed in the local park.
>
>*Several benches in the park are damaged*, and *some of them have loose or broken wooden boards*. This could be dangerous, especially *for children and older residents who use the park regularly*. Since the park is an important space in our neighborhood, I think it is important to address this issue as soon as possible.
>
>I suggest that the association *contact the local maintenance department and ask them to repair or replace the damaged benches*. If additional help is needed, *we could also organize a neighbor volunteer event to help clean up the area around them*.
>
Thank you for considering my suggestion. I hope we can make our park safer and more comfortable for everyone.
Best regards,
Li

**案例点评：**
该篇文章总体来说写得不错。在结构上，文章采取`描述事实`+`评价事实`+`提供信息`的结构完成了题目规定的任务。这里有一些比较好的结构衔接句子可以进行学习，当我们描述并且评价完一个事实之后，准备进入到解决方案的信息提供时，可以这样进行过度
- Since the park is an important space in our community, I think it is important to address this issue as soon as possible（既然这个公园这么重要，我建议我们立刻采取行动来解决这个问题）

在表达上，我们可以积累相关表述。例如描述事实部分，描述公园设施的老旧可以说：
- Several benches are damanged and some of them have loose and broken wooden boards. （一些长椅已经损坏了，另外一些的木板已经有些松弛或者损坏了。）
在解决方案的内容部分，实际上提出了两个方案来应对设备维护问题，可以积累这些表达
- contact the local maintenance department and aske *them* to repair or replace the damaged benches（我建议我们可以联系当地的设备维护部门，让它们来维修或者更换受损的设备）
- organize a volunteer event to clean up the working area.（组织一些志愿者来清理工作现场）

>**Prompt:**
>You are a student organizing a study group for an upcoming exam. You want to invite your classmates to join the group and explain to them your ideas about how the group should proceed.
> 
>Write an email to Alex. In your email, do the following.
> • Explain the purpose of the study group.

In addition, each student could prepare a few questions before meeting so that we can discuss them as a group.
>
> For our first meeting, I suggest we meet this Friday at 4 p.m. in the university library. It is quiet and convenient for everyone, and *we should have space to study together.*
>
 >Please let me know if you would like to join us. I hope you can come.
> Best,
> Li

**文章点评：**
从结构上看，这篇文章采取的是`评价事实`+`分享信息`+`分享信息`的组织模式，第一段陈述了这个组织的成立目的，是为了帮助学生更好的复习，以及更好的准备期末考试。第二段讲述了组织的计划安排，在学习的时候应该做些什么事情；第三段讲述了接下来的计划安排。

从内容上看，关于一个学习小组的成立目的，我们可以积累这些内容。
- the main purpose is to review important course material and help each other prepare more effectively for the final exam. （主要目的是为了复习到课堂上的关键内容，以及帮助每个人更好的准备期末考试）

关于一个学习小组的建议活动，可以积累
- focus on the *material covered in the class*, especially the chapters that *are likely to appear on the final exam*.（复习课堂内容，尤其是那些可能考到的内容）
- review difficult concepts, compare notes, and work on practice questions together.（一起复习难点、比较笔记、做练习题） 

关于学习地点的内容，可以做如下积累：
- be quiet and convenient for everyone
- have enough space to study together

>

Since there are many reliable sources available on this topic, and I have also done some research on one specific metal compound in our laboratory. *This background would allow me* support my presentation with stronger evidence and *explain the topic more clearly*.

案例总结：
在结构上，本案采取三段论的方式组织论述。这里我们需要注意到在结论处有一个新表达：这些经历让我能够（This background would allow me ...）。

在词汇上，本案需要积累一些学术研究方面的词：可信的证据（reliable evidence）、展示变得不可信（make presentation less convincing）、更好的解释主题（explain the topic more clearly）

在措辞上，本案仍然需要关注语法表达习惯。首先research+on是固定搭配。其次evidence一般不可数，因此需要用little搭配而不是few。再次是间接疑问句I am wondering 后面是陈述形式表达疑问，结尾采用句号而不是疑问号；最后在假设部分表达潜在的好处时不应该用This helps me...而是用This would help me ...；

**案例点评：**
从结构上看，本案依旧采取三段论论述。开头采取直抒胸臆的起手式，一句开头直接表明本次email的主要情况；
- I may arrive late to the workshop

结尾一句简直就是这种道歉文章的典型打法，简直是百试不爽。与此同时，三段论尽量将最重要的一段安排在最后再说，这样可以突出重点，让文章更有逻辑感。（傻福ETS）
- apologize for any inconvenience this may cause


从措辞上看，关于我迟到了的表达有：
- 我可能迟到了（I may arrive late/ I may not arrive on time/）、我迟到的这种行为（my late arrival）。
关于我迟到的后果可以有：
- 干扰了活动节奏（disrupt the schedule）、让观众一头雾水（confuse the attendees）
关于迟到问题的解决可以说：
- That will help evething start smoothly.
对于搭档的介绍，也有很地道的表达：
- 我们搭档过很多次了（we have been partners at many events）
