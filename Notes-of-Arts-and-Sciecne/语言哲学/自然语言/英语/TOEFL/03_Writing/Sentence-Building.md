# 疑问句
## 一般疑问句
> Q:
> I'm planning to go to the beach tomorrow.
> A:
> What is water temperature like this time of the year?

固定搭配：
What is the water temperature like? ::: What temperature is the water?

## 复合疑问句
间接疑问句内部必须恢复成**陈述句语序**，也就是疑问词+主谓宾的形式。

> **Example:1**
> 直接疑问句：
> How many classes are we allowed to miss?
> 复合疑问句：
> Do you know [how many classes we are allowed to miss]?

> **Example2:**
> Q:
> Do you understand the main point if the lecture?
> A:
> Yes, I now understand what the professor's main argument was.

> Q:
> The new art exhibit at the museum is stunning.
> A:
> Would you ***happend to know*** ***how I can get*** exhibition information?

happen to know::经典复合疑问句起手式
<!--SR:!2026-08-18,3,250-->
how I can get::经典复合疑问部分（不倒装）

# 陈述句
## 主系表
> Example1:
> Q:
> There are several tasks due this week.
> A:
> Then you should decide ***which tasks are urgent*** to handle first.


## 虚拟语气
> Q:
> I mean, the dust and noise won't be coming directly into our windows.
> A:
> You know I also ***wish*** it ***weren't*** being done.

## 从句
> Q:
> Where should this paragraph go?
> A:
> It fits best in the discussion section *since* it interprets results.


## 复合从句

> Q:
> I emailed the professor about my topic.
> A:
> Good.Now you know what articles *you should read* before writing.
