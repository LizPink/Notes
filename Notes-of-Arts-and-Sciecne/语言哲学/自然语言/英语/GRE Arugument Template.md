# Introduction
## Hook
- Introduce the topic that you want to talk about
- Anecdote, interesting fact, trend, quote, etc.
## Shift to Prompt
- A cohesive shift from hook to your essay's topic.
## Thesis or Stance
- The thesis or stance you choose on the spectrum.
- Prefer "mostly agree" or "mostly disagree".
## Outline
- Demonstrate your arrangement of the rest of the essay.
